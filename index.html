Meet Our Visionary

Shiv Malviya

Founder & Managing Director

Shiv Malviya founded Shivalay Infratech in 2012 with a vision to provide quality homes to families in Indore. With over 13 years of excellence in real estate development, he has established the company as one of the most trusted names in Indore's property market.

Under his leadership, Shivalay Infratech has delivered 25+ successful projects, providing dream homes to over 500 happy families. Every project reflects his commitment to 100% RERA compliance, transparent business practices, and customer satisfaction.

His dedication to quality, ethical practices, and modern amenities has made Shivalay Infratech synonymous with reliability in Indore's real estate sector.
